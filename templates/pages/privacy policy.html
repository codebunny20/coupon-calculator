Privacy Policy

Coupon Calculator is designed to keep things simple and local. The app is intended for personal convenience and does not require an account or personal login.

What is stored

  • Recent calculation history is stored locally on this device in a JSON file.
  • Theme preferences may be saved in the browser using localStorage so the interface remembers light or dark mode.

What is not collected

We do not require an email address, user profile, or account creation. We do not sell personal data, and the app does not track your shopping habits across unrelated websites.

Third-party services

The calculator itself does not use advertising trackers or analytics to profile users. If this app is hosted by a service provider, standard hosting logs may still exist as part of normal website operations, but no personal shopping data is intentionally collected for marketing or advertising purposes.

Your control

You can clear saved history in the app settings and remove browser storage if you want to reset the local data stored for this tool. This helps keep the app privacy-friendly and easy to manage.

By using Coupon Calculator, you agree that it is intended for convenience and quick estimate checking rather than formal accounting, legal advice, or retailer-specific policy interpretation.
