Coupon Calculator helps you estimate savings before checkout, compare multibuy deals, and review your recent results without doing the math in your head.

Core tools

What this app can do

  • Calculate a percentage or fixed-amount discount.
  • Compare regular price versus deal price for multibuy offers.
  • Store recent calculation history locally for quick review.
Best for

Everyday shopping

  • Sales and clearance discounts.
  • Buy-one-get-one promotions.
  • Bundle pricing and special offer comparisons.

1. Calculate a standard discount

  1. 1 Enter the original price, such as $79.99.
  2. 2 Pick either a percentage discount or a fixed discount.
  3. 3 Add the discount value and review the result summary.
  4. 4 Check the final price, total savings, and percentage saved.
Example A $120 item with a 25% discount saves $30 and brings the final price to $90.
Discount amount = original price × discount % / 100

2. Use the multibuy deal calculator

The multibuy section helps compare a normal purchase with a bundled or promotional deal.

Deal type

Buy X get Y free

Example: buy 2, get 1 free. The calculator estimates how many units you actually pay for and how much you save.

Deal type

X for Y items

Example: 3 for 2 or 4 for 3. This is useful when a promotion pays for fewer items than the bundle contains.

Deal type

X for fixed price

Example: 2 items for $5. This is useful for deals that are priced as a bundle instead of a percentage.

The app compares:

  • Regular total at normal unit pricing
  • Deal total under the promotion
  • Amount saved
  • Effective unit price after the deal

3. Validation rules and safety checks

  • Negative values are rejected.
  • Percentage discounts cannot be greater than 100%.
  • Fixed discounts are capped to the original price so the final total cannot go below zero.
  • Quantity must be at least 1 for multibuy calculations.
  • Bundle and deal values must be valid numbers before the app will calculate a result.

4. Quick tips for accurate results

  • Use the plus and minus buttons to test small pricing differences quickly.
  • Match the discount type to the offer: percentage for sale percentages, fixed amount for dollar-off deals.
  • Check whether a bundle deal is being offered as “buy X get Y free,” “X for Y items,” or “X for fixed price.”
  • Use recent history to compare multiple deal scenarios before buying.

5. Troubleshooting

If a result looks unrealistic, double-check the following:

  • The price and discount fields are valid numbers.
  • The correct discount type is selected.
  • The deal mix matches the kind of offer you are modeling.
  • The bundle quantities and price are realistic for the promotion.

For simple pricing checks, start with price + discount. For promotion-heavy purchases, switch to the multibuy calculator and compare the regular total to the deal total.
