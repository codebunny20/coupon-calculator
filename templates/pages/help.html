Help

Use Coupon Calculator to estimate savings before you buy.

How to calculate a discount

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Choose either a percentage discount or a fixed amount discount.
  3. Enter the discount value.
  4. Review the final price, total savings, and discount summary.

Quick tips

  • Use the plus and minus buttons for small adjustments when comparing close price points.
  • Check that your discount amount is not greater than the original price.
  • Use the multibuy calculator for deals like bundle pricing, buy-one-get-one promotions, or special group offers.
  • Saved results can be reviewed from the history panel in the main calculator.

If the result seems unrealistic, make sure the values are valid numbers and that the discount type matches the deal you are trying to model.
