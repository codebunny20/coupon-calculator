About Coupon Calculator

Coupon Calculator is a quick, practical tool for checking discounts while shopping, comparing offers, and deciding whether a sale or bundle is really worth it.

Built for busy checkout moments, the app helps shoppers and retail staff estimate the true savings on a purchase before they commit to a deal.

What it does

  • Calculates percentage and fixed-amount discounts.
  • Shows the reduced total and the money saved.
  • Supports multibuy promotions such as buy X get Y free, bundle offers, and X for Y deals.
  • Tracks recent calculations locally so users can review past totals quickly.

Why it exists

Many deals look more appealing than they are, especially when discounts are stacked or a bundle changes the effective unit price. This calculator makes those decisions clearer and faster.

It uses careful decimal-based math so totals stay accurate and easy to trust, without needing a spreadsheet or manual arithmetic.
