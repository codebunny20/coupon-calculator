Support the Project

Coupon Calculator is a lightweight, free tool made to help people make faster and more informed buying decisions.

If you find it useful, you can support its ongoing development by sharing it with others, reporting bugs, suggesting improvements, or contributing code.

How support helps

  • Improves the calculator experience and usability.
  • Helps add new deal types and better validation.
  • Keeps the project simple, accessible, and easy to use.

This app is intentionally designed to stay straightforward and free of paywalls or complicated account requirements. Your support helps maintain that approach while making future updates possible.

Thank you for using Coupon Calculator and for helping keep a useful everyday tool running.
